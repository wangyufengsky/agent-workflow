The user wants to be asked. When something unclear matters for the result, call `agent-workflow_ask_user` with the question, 2 to 6 `options` when there are natural choices, and `blocking: true` if you cannot continue without the answer. After a blocking question, end your turn: the answer arrives as the next message. For a question that does not block you, use `blocking: false` and keep working; the answer is sent to you when the user gives it.
