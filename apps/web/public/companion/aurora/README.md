# 极光光球素材

2026-10-23 使用内置 ImageGen 按项目需求生成，透明 PNG；与 dsh-pet 鲸鱼素材无关。

`aurora.png` 同时用于静态降级和 WebGL 光带纹理。运行时做非均匀扭动、呼吸、色相漂移；不把 GIF 或视频当作动态状态源。

## 生成提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: visual design reference for a live animated desktop AI assistant.
Primary request: A single exquisite futuristic aurora energy orb, alive and soft, formed entirely from flowing luminous ribbons and translucent nebula filaments. Its almost spherical silhouette is organic and subtly uneven, with airy strands folding around an open luminous core, suggesting continual breathing, flowing and shape morphing. Vibrant cyan, teal, electric blue and violet gradually blend, with tiny restrained rose highlights. Bright silky edges, rich inner depth, a few delicate particles close to the form. Elegant and highly legible at small desktop companion scale.
Scene/backdrop: fully transparent background, no floor, no shadow plane, no frame.
Composition: one orb centered, occupying about 72% of the square canvas, all outer glow and wisps comfortably inside frame.
Lighting: emissive aurora ribbons, soft bloom, luminous without a blown out white center.
Constraints: no face, no text, no logos, no UI, no rigid wireframe rings, no chrome, no plastic/glass marble, no dark opaque sphere. It must look like flowing light itself, not a ball with a texture pasted on.

```
